Wisp Tracking is a very simple GPS tracker for any activity — running, cycling, walking, or anything else. It doesn't care what you're doing, only where you went.

WHAT IT DOES
- Tap Start and Wisp Tracking records your route, distance, and speed.
- See your current speed, distance, and elapsed time live while you move, with your position clearly marked on the map.
- Pause and resume a recording without breaking the route.
- Every activity is saved to a local history on your phone — no account needed.
- Open any past activity to see its full route, distance, duration, and average/max speed.
- Export your whole history as a CSV file for a spreadsheet, or export a single activity as an image to share.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO
Wisp Tracking is deliberately simple. There's no activity-type picker, no cadence or heart-rate tracking, no social feed, no accounts, and no cloud sync. Your data stays on your device.

PERMISSIONS
Wisp Tracking asks for location access only when you start recording, and explains why before the system prompt appears. Background location is optional — without it, recording may stop if you leave the app while tracking.

Wisp Tracking is open source. Feedback, bug reports, and feature requests are welcome at the project's GitHub issues page.
